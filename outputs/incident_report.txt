Security Incident Report

Incident Title: Suspected Brute-Force Attack and Unauthorized Access
Date: 2026-04-01
Source: System Authentication & Network Logs

1. Summary
A sequence of failed authentication attempts followed by a successful login to the admin account was observed from an internal IP address. Concurrently, repeated external connection attempts targeting RDP (port 3389) were detected. Shortly after, a new guest account was created. The activity indicates a potential compromise and unauthorized system access.

2. Observed Activity
- Multiple failed login attempts for admin from 192.168.1.45
- Successful login for admin from the same IP
- Repeated inbound connection attempts to port 3389 (RDP) from 203.0.113.17
- Creation of a guest account

3. Analysis
- The authentication pattern suggests a brute-force or password guessing attack
- The successful login raises concern of credential compromise
- External RDP probing indicates potential reconnaissance or access attempts
- The creation of a new account suggests possible persistence establishment

4. Impact Assessment
- Risk Level: High
- Potential unauthorized access to privileged account
- Risk of lateral movement and further system compromise

5. Recommended Actions
1. Disable or lock the admin account immediately
2. Reset credentials for all privileged users
3. Investigate host associated with 192.168.1.45
4. Block or restrict traffic from 203.0.113.17
5. Review and disable the newly created guest account
6. Conduct full log review for:
   - Privilege escalation
   - Lateral movement
   - Data access or exfiltration
7. Enforce account lockout policies
8. Restrict RDP access (VPN or IP allowlisting)

6. Confidence Level
Medium
The activity strongly aligns with known attack patterns; however, confirmation of full compromise requires further investigation.
